WHAT IS QUINTET?

Core Rule

Teams of 5. Winner stays on the mat and faces the next opponent. Single-elimination with consolation matches.

Match Time

6 minutes. Points start after 3 minutes.

Win Streak

Winners stay on the mat for up to 3 consecutive wins.

Draws

Both competitors are eliminated.

Mat Order

The order in which competitors step onto the mat is determined by weight, in ascending order: the lightest competitors go first. You are not guaranteed to face your own weight class.

How a Team Wins

Last team with a competitor left on the mat wins.

ADCC RULE SET CHEAT SHEET

Legal Leg Locks

Straight ankle locks, kneebars, toe holds, calf & bicep slicers.

Banned Submissions

Heel hooks (inside & outside), neck cranks / can openers, twisters / spine locks.

Slams

Strictly illegal.
